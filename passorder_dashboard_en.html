Key Drivers Dismantling Franchise Lock-In

  • Control Group (Group A): Stamps were fragmented by store, offering zero incentive to explore alternative brands, leading users to patronize only single local stores (Cross-brand order rate: 17.8%).
  • Treatment Group (Group B): Points earned at Mega Coffee could be immediately deducted like cash at Compose or Paik's Coffee, surging the cross-brand order rate to 48.3% (2.7x lift).
